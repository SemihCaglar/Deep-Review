## Summary of Contribution
The paper proposes SERSEM, a selective entropy-weighted scoring method for membership inference attacks. It aims to improve the detection of whether a data point was part of a model’s training set by leveraging entropy-based weighting. The approach is evaluated in the context of membership inference, but details are sparse.
