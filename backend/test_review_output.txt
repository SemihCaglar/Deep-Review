## Paper Title
SERSEM: Selective Entropy-Weighted Scoring for Membership Inference in Code Language Models

## Overall Decision
Weak Accept

## Summary of Contribution
The paper introduces SERSEM, a novel selective entropy-weighted scoring method for membership inference attacks on code language models. It aims to improve the accuracy and reliability of detecting whether a data sample was part of a model’s training set, specifically in the context of code generation models.

## Critical Review
### Methodology & Validity
The methodology centers on entropy-weighted scoring, but the paper lacks sufficient detail on experimental setup, dataset selection, and baseline comparisons. The validity of the results is difficult to fully assess due to limited reporting of statistical significance and ablation studies. The approach appears promising, but more rigorous evaluation is needed to confirm its effectiveness over existing methods.

### Industrial Relevance
Membership inference attacks are highly relevant for privacy and intellectual property concerns in deployed code language models. The proposed method could be valuable for practitioners seeking to audit or defend against such attacks, but the paper does not provide concrete case studies or deployment scenarios to demonstrate real-world impact.

### Clarity & Presentation
The writing suffers from formatting issues and inconsistent sectioning, making it challenging to follow the argument and experimental results. Key terms and concepts are not always clearly defined, and the structure does not guide the reader through the contributions and findings effectively.

## Prioritized Improvements
### High Priority
- Provide a clear, structured experimental section with detailed methodology, datasets, and baseline comparisons.
- Improve the clarity and consistency of writing, including section headings and definitions of key terms.

### Medium Priority
- Include statistical analysis and ablation studies to strengthen claims of effectiveness.
- Add real-world scenarios or case studies to illustrate industrial relevance.

### Low Priority
- Address formatting issues and typographical errors throughout the paper.
- Enhance figures and tables for better readability.

## Scope Analysis
### Underemphasized Areas
- Real-world deployment considerations and defenses against membership inference attacks.
- Detailed comparison with state-of-the-art methods in both code and natural language domains.

### Overemphasized Areas
- Theoretical motivation for entropy-weighted scoring receives more attention than empirical validation and practical implications.
